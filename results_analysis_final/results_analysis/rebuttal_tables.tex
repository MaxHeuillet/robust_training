\toprule
Backbone & TRADES ($\beta=1$) & Classic AT & TRADES ($\beta=6$) \\

eva02\_base\_patch14\_224.mim\_in22k      & \textbf{17.5995} & 13.1634 & 13.9792 \\

\end{tabular}

\vspace{2pt}
\footnotesize
Note: TRADES ($\beta=6$) is never the best-performing loss for any of the 5
backbones (0/5 wins) -- it underperforms both TRADES ($\beta=1$) and Classic AT
uniformly, so the mean is not an average-only artifact. TRADES ($\beta=1$) and
Classic AT are closer, but not equivalent, and the direction is
backbone-dependent: TRADES ($\beta=1$) wins for 3/5 backbones (eva02\_base,
vit\_base, vit\_small), while Classic AT wins for 2/5 (deit\_small, swin\_tiny) --
evidence against a single homogeneous ``TRADES $\approx$ Classic AT for
attention architectures'' story at the individual-backbone level, even though
it may hold approximately on average.

\vspace{4pt}
\emph{Limitation:} the $\beta=6$ runs reuse the hyperparameters (learning
rates, weight decay) selected by the $\beta=1$ HPO search rather than an
independent search at $\beta=6$, consistent with this benchmark's
fixed-HPO-per-configuration evaluation protocol throughout. Since TRADES loss
is CE $+ \beta \cdot$KL(robust, natural), a 6$\times$ larger $\beta$
substantially changes the loss landscape, so part of the $\beta=6$
underperformance could reflect a hyperparameter mismatch rather than a pure
effect of $\beta$ alone. We report this as a directional finding, not a
fully isolated causal claim about $\beta$.
\end{table}
